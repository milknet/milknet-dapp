Frequently Asked Questions
What is your sales process?

We use a 5 step sales process that is designed to fully understand your current operations and the potential benefits Milk Moovement can offer you. Our 5 step process is completely free and ends with a custom proposal for your team to review.
The process includes:

Needs/Problems Assessment
Current System Evaluation
Gather Requirements
Milestone Planning
Proposal
How much does the system cost?

We charge by the Liters, Kilograms or pounds of raw milk/processed milk managed. Prices range based on quantity managed on an annual basis.

Do you build custom features?

We do! As a growth company, it is our goal to create the best product possible with features that support all clients. In our sales process we will do a deep dive into your current system and evaluate all the custom features required to make Milk Moovement the perfect system for you. These can include reports, functions, or totally new feature sets.

How long does it take to get setup?

With minimal customization, we can have everything set up and running for you in less than a month. However, larger co-operatives typically go through our on-boarding process found here.

What new features are you working on?

We do monthly updates on our product. To join the list and see what we are working on, click here.
